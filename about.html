Our Story

VT Travel was created from a simple idea: holiday planning should feel exciting, personal and stress-free.

We know choosing a holiday can feel overwhelming. There are thousands of destinations, hotels, flights and options — and it can be difficult to know whether you've found the right one.

That's where we come in. As an independent travel agent, we take the time to understand you — where you want to go, how you like to travel, what's important to you and your budget.

We don't believe in treating customers like a booking reference. We believe in getting to know the people behind the holiday.

Our Mission

To make every holiday feel personal, exciting and effortless.

We want VT Travel to be the travel agent you come back to — not just because we found you a great holiday, but because we remembered what mattered to you.

Your journey starts long before you reach the airport. We're here to make every part of it count.
