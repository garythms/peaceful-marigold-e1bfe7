Your Data

Privacy notice

What we collect, why we collect it, who sees it and how long we keep it.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

VT Travel is an independent travel agent affiliated with Elevana Travel. We are the controller of the personal information you give us through this website. Contact us at info@vttravel.co.uk or on 01608 670152.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, preferred airport, who is travelling (including children's ages), any special occasion, your requirements and your marketing choice. If you enquire from a particular holiday deal, we also record which deal it was.

If you later book with us we also need the details the airline, hotel or tour operator requires, such as passport names and dates of birth. We collect those directly from you when needed, not through the website enquiry form.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your trip. This is needed to take steps at your request before a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep records we are required to keep, for example for accounting and financial protection (legal obligation).
  • To send you holiday ideas and offers, only if you tick the box to hear from us. You can withdraw that at any time (consent).
  • To run and protect our business, such as keeping our systems secure and responding to complaints (legitimate interests).

Who sees it

Only the people who work at VT Travel. We share your details with airlines, hotels and tour operators only when you ask us to quote or book, because they need them to provide your holiday. We do not sell your details.

We use these service providers to run our systems, and they handle data only on our instructions: DigitalOcean (secure server hosting in London), Telegram (a private alert to our phones that a new enquiry has arrived), and Netlify (hosts this website).

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are kept for up to 2 years, then deleted. If you book, we keep your records for up to 6 years after your holiday, in line with accounting rules and the time limit for legal claims. If you ask us to stop marketing, we keep only enough to make sure we do not contact you.

Your rights

You can ask us to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or give it to you in a portable form. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time. Email info@vttravel.co.uk and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

Our records are held on a private, access-controlled server. Staff sign in with a password and a second verification step, connections are encrypted and the data is backed up regularly.

Cookies and weather

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The clocks and weather on the home page ask a free weather service (Open-Meteo) for the weather in a few cities. Your browser contacts it directly, so it will see your IP address, but we send it no personal details.

Changes

We will update this page if how we use your information changes, and change the date at the top.
